Overview

Kemerya Tours created this private day tour for the travelers who want to travel over 4,500 years back in time. You can start your tour on the top world-renowned site, the Giza Plateau, which houses one of the most remarkable landmarks in the world, including the Great Pyramid of Khufu, the Pyramid of Khafre, the Pyramid of Menkaure, and the famous Sphinx, and enjoy taking a panoramic photos and learn the history that each place is telling you through your professional Egyptologist guide. Move on to the necropolises that ancient Egyptians used to bury the most important persons in society. That place, which we call Saqqara. Where will you witness one of the most important and ancient monuments in Egypt namely the Step pyramid of King Djoser, which is considered as the first large-stone construction that existed in history, just like that it is the base for building pyramid at Giza site you also would enjoy exploring the tombs that surrounds pyramid and know all facts about pyramids' history.

At the end of the tour you would reach Memphis, the first capital of ancient Egypt, where it housed a group of astonishing statues, like that giant statue of Ramses 2.

And you can also find the Alabaster Sphinx, one of the most famous and largest sphinx statues of ancient times, and also a number of interesting and old artifacts in the open-air museum, the place where one of the biggest cities existed.

Tour Plan

  • Giza Pyramids: we’ll start our journey on the Giza Pyramids to explore the history of the Giza Pyramids, including King Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure. Each pyramid has its own story, and our Egyptologist tour guide will inform you of the history of each pyramid.
  • Great Sphinx: Then we’ll see the Great Sphinx. Standing beside the Giza Pyramids. It’s the face of a pharaoh with a lion body. And it is considered one of the greatest symbols of ancient Egypt.
  • Lunchtime: you’ll eat one of the most delicious Egyptian foods in Cairo, take a break, and enjoy your delicious lunch before continuing our journey.
  • Saqqara: We’ll continue to Saqqara. It was designed by the architect Imhotep. And it’s the home to the famous Step Pyramid of King Djoser.
  • Memphis: We’ll end the day in Memphis, the first capital of ancient Egypt. The site is home to remarkable statues and archaeological remains, including the Colossal Statue of Ramses II, offering a glimpse into one of Egypt’s oldest and most important cities.
