Cairo Day Tours

Best Cairo Excursions & Private Sightseeing Tours

Experience the beauty and excitement of Egypt's capital by taking our top-rated Cairo day trips. Whether you're traveling to Cairo for the first time or you want to revisit the country's capital and see even more history and culture, our tours are ideal to experience many ofCairo's iconic landmarks within a single day. In our Cairo tours you will see the most imposing Giza Pyramids and its guardianSphinx, explore thousands of spectacular works at the new Grand Egyptian Museum and walk in the lanes of Old Cairo including most famous churches and mosques, get to enjoy the traditional local shopping experience at Khan El Khalili Market.

Our tours include a licenced Egyptologist guide and will take you to the historical sites to explore Egypt’s rich past, your accommodation pick-up and drop-off, air-conditioned transport and a complete guided tour itinerary.

Available tours include private tours or small group tours, we haveCairo day tours catered to any interest, including those interested in Egyptian antiquities, Islamic and Coptic culture and history, as well as fantastic photography locations. Choose a Cairo day tour today and begin making memories at Egypt's enthralling capital.

Frequently asked questions

Are your tours private or shared in groups?

All of our standard and custom day tours are strictly private. This means you will have your own dedicated vehicle, driver, and personal Egyptologist guide, allowing you to move at your own pace without being rushed by a large tour group.

What is included in your Egypt tour packages?

Most of our packages include private professional Egyptologist guides, entry tickets to major sights mentioned in the itinerary, private air-conditioned transportation, and select meals or hotel accommodations depending on the specific tour you choose.

 

Is it safe to travel to Egypt?

Yes, Egypt is a popular and safe destination for international travelers. Major tourist areas, historical sites, and hotels have strong security measures in place. Our private tours also include professional guides and secure, private transportation to ensure a smooth and worry-free experience from the moment you arrive.

 

Can I customize my itinerary?

Absolutely. We specialize in tailoring trips to match your preferences, schedule, and budget. Whether you want to add extra days in Luxor, modify your hotel choices, or include specific activities, our team will design a custom itinerary just for you.

 

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. We use secure payment gateways and encryption to keep your personal and payment information safe.

 

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes, most bookings can be changed or canceled, depending on the airline, hotel, or service provider’s policy. Please check the cancellation terms before confirming your booking.

How do I book a trip on your website?

You can easily book by selecting your destination, choosing your travel dates, and following the checkout steps. A confirmation will be sent to your email once the booking is complete.
